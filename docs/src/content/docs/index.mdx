Harpy is a [linked-read](Getting_Started/inputformat.md) and non-linked WGS data processing pipeline for Linux-based systems.
It uses [Snakemake](https://snakemake.readthedocs.io/en/stable/) under the hood to handle the worklfow decision-making, 
but users just interact with it like a normal command-line program. Harpy employs both well known and niche programs to
take raw linked-read sequences and process them to become called SNP genotypes (or haplotypes) or large structural variants
(inversions, deletions, duplications). Feel free to open an [Issue](https://github.com/blinkseq/harpy/issues/new/choose)
or begin a [Discussion](https://github.com/blinkseq/harpy/discussions) on GitHub.
